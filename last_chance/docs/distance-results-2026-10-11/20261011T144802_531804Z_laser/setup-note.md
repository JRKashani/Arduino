# Laser alignment change reported after collection

The operator reported that the laser was tilted back and was straightened around
the 60 cm position. The exact transition (before, during or after its repeats)
has not been established. Treat this run as potentially containing two mounting
conditions; do not fit a single calibration across them without resolving this.

Entered 70 cm produced a mean 71.90 cm; 60 cm produced 62.30 cm; 50 cm produced
51.41 cm. These measurements alone do not locate or prove the alignment change.
Repeat 70, 60 and 50 cm with the sensor fixed in its final orientation, using
three captures per distance and measuring from the sensor face. For a calibration
covering the full interval, repeat the full interval with that fixed orientation.

Raw events and existing exports are unchanged. This note records context only;
no captures have been excluded and no geometric correction has been applied.
